# MR12: FX, Komoditas & Basis Risk

Risiko FX (transaksi/translasi/ekonomi), komoditas (spot vs futures, cost of carry, contango/backwardation), dan basis risk — sisa risiko setelah hedging.

- Transaksi -> forward; belum pasti -> opsi; struktural -> strategi bisnis
- F = S*e^((r+u-y)T); backwardation = pasar panik kekurangan barang
- Basis = F - S; P&L ter-hedge = -d(Basis)
- Hedge = tukar risiko harga -> risiko basis (+ likuiditas margin)
- h* = rho*s_S/s_F dari data, bukan asumsi 1:1
