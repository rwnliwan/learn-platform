# MR16: Regulasi — Basel 2.5 & FRTB

Evolusi regulasi modal risiko pasar: Basel 2.5 (stressed VaR, IRC) -> FRTB (boundary ketat, ES 97.5%, liquidity horizon, SBM/IMA, NMRF, PLA).

- Stressed VaR = obat amnesia model
- FRTB: SBM (sensitivitas) vs IMA (model internal per desk)
- ES 97.5% + liquidity horizon (10-120 hari) = inti modal
- IMA lolos 2 ujian: backtest + PLA (Spearman & KS)
- NMRF: <24 harga real/tahun -> stress charge tanpa diversifikasi
- Regulasi = arsitektur insentif
